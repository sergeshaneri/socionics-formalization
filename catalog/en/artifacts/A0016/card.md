# Enumeration of generators of fractal inheritance

## Description

The report describes a complete enumeration of compatible generators of fractal inheritance, criteria for accepting configurations, and factorized storage of results.

## Claimed results

- The README claims an enumeration of 20,160 ordered independent quadruples of traits, 168 Aspecton triples, and 16 orientations over a space of 54,190,080 configurations; outcomes are stored in 322,560 rows of outcomes.csv. **Basis:** README.md, introductory “Result” section and §§2–6.
