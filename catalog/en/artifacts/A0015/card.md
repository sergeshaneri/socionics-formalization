# Products of 16 trait systems: waffle octads and pairings

## Description

The report examines pairwise products of trait systems, the induced octads, and 15 ways to partition 16 systems into pairs. It highlights a connection between one pairing and inversion of Model A relations.

## Claimed results

- The report claims that 120 pairs of distinct systems yield 15 octads of additional traits, each arising from eight pairs; it also presents further classes and connections to tetrachotomies. **Basis:** introductory result; §§3–11.
